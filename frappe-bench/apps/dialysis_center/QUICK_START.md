## 📊 Available Modules

1. **Patient Management** - Patient records, demographics
2. **Appointment Scheduling** - Book and manage appointments
3. **Equipment Management** - Dialysis machines tracking
4. **Clinical Operations** - Dialysis sessions, vital signs, templates
5. **Laboratory Management** - Lab tests and results
6. **Billing & Financial** - Invoices and payments
7. **Staff Management** - Staff records and scheduling
8. **Inventory Management** - Medications and stock
9. **Insurance Management** - Insurance providers and claims

## 📈 Available Reports

- Patient Treatment History
- Machine Utilization Report
- Financial Summary
